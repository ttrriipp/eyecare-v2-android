# Eyecare Android App — User-Centered Demo Video

## Recommended concept

Tell the story entirely from a patient's perspective, beginning with their first contact with the app. The viewer should always understand:

1. what the patient is trying to do;
2. what action they take;
3. what the app makes possible next; and
4. which decisions still belong to the clinic.

- **Target length:** 7–8 minutes
- **Style:** descriptive narrated screen recording centered on the patient journey
- **Main path:** Create account → connect clinic record → Home → Frames and AR → appointment request → confirmed care → orders, prescriptions, and messages → settings
- **Primary message:** “Eyecare helps patients manage their relationship with Padilla Optical Clinic from an Android phone.”

## How to present additional possibilities

Keep one clear main path. When the app offers another route, show a two- or three-second lower-third instead of leaving the journey to demonstrate it.

Examples:

- **Already registered?** Sign in or recover the password.
- **No invitation code?** Continue with a safe unlinked account and ask the clinic to review a link request later.
- **Not linked yet?** Browse frames and submit an appointment request; protected clinical records remain unavailable.
- **No AR model available?** View the product images instead.
- **After clinic confirmation:** Reschedule or cancel eligible appointments.
- **After a completed visit:** Rate the experience.

This informs viewers about the app's range without turning the video into disconnected feature demonstrations.

## Shot-by-shot flow and script

### 0:00–0:18 — Start from the patient's need

**On screen**

- Fade in to the Eyecare Welcome screen.
- Keep both **Sign in** and **Create account** visible.
- Tap **Create account**.

**Voice-over**

> The patient journey begins on the Welcome screen. A new user can create an Eyecare account to discover eyewear, manage clinic visits, and access eligible records and services from one place.

**Possibility callout:** “Returning patients can sign in or recover their password.”

### 0:18–1:18 — Create and verify the account

**On screen**

- Enter a synthetic Philippine mobile number.
- Request the verification code.
- Enter the six-digit OTP; hide or blur the actual code.
- Continue to **Your details**.
- Fill in the patient's synthetic name and optional email.
- Enter a password slowly enough to show the inline requirements becoming valid:
  - eight characters;
  - uppercase and lowercase letters;
  - a number;
  - a special character;
  - matching confirmation.
- Open the in-app Terms of Service briefly, return, and do the same for the Privacy Policy.
- Accept both agreements.

**Voice-over**

> Account creation begins by verifying ownership of a phone number using a six-digit code. The patient then provides the required account details. While the password is entered, the app displays each security requirement immediately and indicates when it has been satisfied. The Terms of Service and Privacy Policy can also be read inside the app before consent is given.

**Recording note:** Record this sequence separately and shorten typing pauses during editing. Never display a real phone number, password, or OTP.

### 1:18–1:48 — Explain the clinic-record connection

**On screen**

- Show **Have an invitation code?** on the registration form.
- If a prepared invitation code is available, enter it and finish registration.
- Otherwise, finish registration without one and briefly show the Home card inviting the user to connect their clinic record.
- Display a simple two-path overlay:

  **Invitation code → verify and link now**  
  **No code → ask the clinic to review later**

**Voice-over**

> The mobile account and the clinic's patient record remain separate until their connection is verified. A patient who has received an invitation code can enter it during registration. A patient without a code can still use the app and ask the clinic to review a link request later. Until the link becomes active, frame browsing and appointment requests remain available, while prescriptions, confirmed appointments, and orders stay protected.

**Transition:** Continue the main demonstration with a prepared linked patient account. If it is not the account just registered, use the title card **“Once the clinic record is linked…”** so the change is honest and easy to follow.

### 1:48–2:18 — Arrive at Home

**On screen**

- Show the linked patient's Home screen.
- Pause on the greeting and **Your next visit** card.
- Scroll gently to clinic hours and featured frames.
- Point briefly to the notification indicator and bottom navigation.

**Voice-over**

> Once the clinic record is linked, Home provides a quick view of what matters next. It presents the patient's upcoming visit, current clinic hours, notifications, and featured frames. The main navigation keeps Home, Frames, Appointments, and Profile within easy reach.

### 2:18–3:25 — Discover frames and use AR try-on

**On screen**

- Open **Frames**.
- Browse the catalog at a readable speed.
- Open a verified AR-ready frame.
- Show its images, name, price, dimensions, and variants.
- Select a variant and tap **Try on**.
- Show the camera view with the frame model tracking the face.
- Move left and right slowly.
- Tap **Save frame**.
- Briefly show Profile → **Saved Frames**, or use a small “Saved for later” confirmation shot.

**Voice-over**

> The Frames section allows patients to explore the clinic's collection before visiting. Each frame presents its images, price, measurements, and available variants. For an AR-ready option, the front camera provides a preview that follows the patient's face as it moves. This serves as a visual guide rather than a final fitting, which still takes place at the clinic. Preferred frames can be saved for later.

**Possibility callout:** “If a 3D model is unavailable, frame images remain accessible.”

**Demo requirement:** Use only a frame and variant whose model has already been verified on the recording device. Keep a successful AR clip as a backup.

### 3:25–4:45 — Request an appointment

**On screen**

- Open **Appointments**.
- Show **Upcoming** and **History**.
- Tap **Request appointment**.
- Complete the guided request:
  1. choose the request type;
  2. select an open day and available time;
  3. select or enter the reason for the visit;
  4. review the entered information;
  5. tap **Send request**.
- Show **Request sent** and its reference number.
- Tap **View request**.
- Return to the list and show the new request appearing automatically.

**Voice-over**

> When a visit is needed, the patient can submit a request through a guided process. The patient chooses the visit type, selects from days and times supplied by the clinic, provides a reason for the visit, and reviews all information before sending.
>
> The app makes an important distinction: this submission is a request, not a confirmed appointment. The preferred time is sent to the clinic for review. A reference number is issued, and the request appears in the appointment list automatically so its status can be followed.

**Possibility callout:** “For an unlinked account, an additional Details step collects the identity information needed for clinic review. The verified phone number cannot be changed here.”

### 4:45–5:28 — Follow the visit from request to completion

**On screen**

- Place a pending request and a confirmed appointment in view.
- Open the pending request detail, then return.
- Open the confirmed appointment detail.
- Show date, time, status, and available actions.
- Open the reschedule sheet briefly without submitting.
- If seeded, show a completed appointment and its rating action.

**Voice-over**

> The appointment list clearly distinguishes a request awaiting clinic action from an appointment that has already been confirmed. A pending request can be reviewed or cancelled. Once the clinic confirms a visit, the final schedule and status become available. Depending on its current state, the appointment may also be rescheduled or cancelled. A completed visit can be rated by the patient.

**Possibility callout:** “Clinic-confirmed schedule • Eligible rescheduling • Cancellation • Post-visit rating”

### 5:28–6:05 — Track eyewear orders

**On screen**

- Go to Profile → **My Orders**.
- Switch between **Current** and **History**.
- Open a current order.
- Show the order number, item summary, status, total, and balance due.

**Voice-over**

> Eyewear orders recorded by the clinic can be tracked through My Orders. Current and historical views separate active work from completed orders. Each order shows its reference, items, fulfillment status, total, and any remaining balance. The clinic system remains the source of these order and payment updates.

**Possibility callout:** “Follow active orders • Review order history • See remaining balance”

**Do not claim:** The patient purchases the frame inside the app. The current demonstrated capability is viewing and tracking clinic-recorded orders.

### 6:05–6:43 — View prescriptions and contact the clinic

**On screen**

- Return to Profile and open **Prescriptions**.
- Open one synthetic prescription detail.
- Return and open **Messages**.
- Show an existing conversation.
- Send a harmless message such as: “Hello, what should patients bring to an appointment?”
- If available, show an appointment or order context card in the conversation.

**Voice-over**

> A linked patient can view eligible prescription history from the phone. When assistance is needed, Messages provides a direct conversation with clinic staff. Appointment or order context can remain attached to the conversation, helping both sides understand exactly what the question concerns.

**Possibility callout:** “Prescription history • Direct clinic messaging • Contextual appointment and order references”

### 6:43–7:12 — Personalize and manage the account

**On screen**

- Return to Profile.
- Show Account details, Patient profile, Saved Frames, My Orders, Prescriptions, and Messages.
- Open **Appearance**.
- Show Light, Dark, and System options; return to Light mode.

**Voice-over**

> Profile brings account management and care activity together. Patients can revisit saved frames, orders, prescriptions, and messages from one place. The app can also use a light or dark appearance or follow the device setting.

### 7:12–7:35 — Close with patient value

**On screen**

- Show a quick recap of the user's saved frame, appointment status, and current order.
- End on the Eyecare logo.
- Final title: **Eyewear. Visits. Clinic connection.**

**Voice-over**

> Eyecare supports the patient journey from account creation and secure clinic linking to eyewear discovery, appointment requests, care information, order tracking, and clinic communication. These services remain accessible from one Android app, while important clinical and operational decisions remain with Padilla Optical Clinic.

## Recommended recording strategy

Record the journey in three clips and edit them into one continuous story:

1. **New-user clip:** Welcome, phone verification, details, password guidance, policies, and clinic-link choices.
2. **Linked-patient clip:** Home, Frames, AR, appointments, orders, prescriptions, messages, and settings.
3. **Backup clip:** A verified AR session and a successful appointment-request submission.

Use the title card **“Once the clinic record is linked…”** between clips one and two. This preserves the patient's chronological story without pretending that a newly created unlinked account already contains clinical history.

## Demo data checklist

- [ ] Use only a synthetic patient identity and contact details.
- [ ] Prepare either a working invitation code or an unlinked-account link-request state.
- [ ] Prepare a linked account with one pending request and one confirmed appointment.
- [ ] Include one completed appointment that can be rated.
- [ ] Include one current order with a balance and one historical order.
- [ ] Include at least one prescription and one existing message thread.
- [ ] Verify a polished AR-ready frame, variant, product image, and 3D model.
- [ ] Remove visible records named “test,” “sample,” or similar development labels.
- [ ] Keep all visible dates and statuses internally consistent.

## Device and rehearsal checklist

- [ ] Use the same physical Android device for rehearsal and recording.
- [ ] Charge the device, enable Do Not Disturb, and hide notification contents.
- [ ] Verify the API and Wi-Fi connection before each take.
- [ ] Grant camera permission before the final AR take unless its explanation is intentionally shown.
- [ ] Clean the camera lens and use even front lighting with a plain background.
- [ ] Keep Light mode, default font size, and default display size throughout the main recording.
- [ ] Record at 1080p or higher and at least 30 fps.
- [ ] Hide passwords, OTPs, real phone numbers, debug overlays, API URLs, and IP addresses.
- [ ] Pause for about one second after each navigation action.
- [ ] Scroll slowly enough for labels and status chips to remain readable.
- [ ] Confirm the appointment list refreshes after successful request creation.
- [ ] Complete one timed rehearsal; target 7:00–7:40.

## Editing guidance

- Keep the portrait recording centered inside a clean 16:9 canvas.
- Add restrained section labels: **Create**, **Connect**, **Discover**, **Request**, and **Manage**.
- Use the app's navy, cyan, and white for title cards.
- Use simple cuts or short dissolves; avoid decorative transition templates.
- Cut loading waits, keyboard corrections, duplicate taps, and unnecessary backtracking.
- Add captions with no more than two lines on screen.
- Use subtle tap indicators if they do not obscure controls.
- Keep music below the narration and reduce it further when explaining account linking or request status.

## Failure-safe alternatives

| If this happens | Recommended response |
|---|---|
| Registration or OTP is delayed | Use the pre-recorded new-user clip and continue with the prepared account. |
| Invitation linking cannot be reseeded | Show the two link possibilities, then transition honestly to a prepared linked account. |
| AR model does not appear | Use the verified AR backup clip; do not present a product photo as an AR overlay. |
| Face tracking is unstable | Improve lighting, use a plain background, center the face, and record a shorter movement. |
| Appointment slot becomes unavailable | Keep a second known-open date and time ready. |
| API or message send is slow | Use a clean backup clip captured from the same synthetic dataset. |
| Sensitive information appears | Stop and record again with synthetic data instead of depending only on blur. |

## Optional clinic-system proof

If the panel expects an end-to-end system demonstration, add 30–45 seconds after the pending appointment request:

1. Freeze on the Android request reference.
2. Show clinic staff finding that same reference in the administration system.
3. Let staff approve or schedule the request using seeded data.
4. Return to Android and show the confirmed result and notification.

**Voice-over**

> The patient submits the request and follows its status in Eyecare. The clinic reviews that same request in its administration system. When staff confirms the schedule, the result returns to the Android app as a confirmed appointment.

Only include this segment after rehearsing both systems together. The Android story should remain understandable even when this optional insert is omitted.
